Procedure:
1. Discover and inspect every source table. Confirm entity, period, grain and exact column names. The server supplies ownership and observed revisions. Financial queries read at most two tables in one snapshot. Summary rows or mixed record types can invalidate a business result even when the arithmetic is exact.
2. Call financial_query with table_id and a query object whose operation is records, lookup, join, reconcile, aging or variance. For two-source operations, query.right_table_id identifies the other inspected table. Never supply source revisions, owner identity or physical bounds.
3. For records, supply columns, optional exact equality filters, offset and limit (at most 100). sort entries require column, kind (number/text/date), direction (asc/desc) and nulls (first/last). Numeric text sorting needs numeric_text=decimal. Sort ties preserve source order. Follow next_offset against the same inspected revisions. matched_rows is the whole result count, not the page size. Returned row coordinates start at one within the registered range, including its header. Numeric cells have type=number and exact string values; text cells stay type=str.
4. For lookup, supply columns, at least one exact equality filter and missing=reject or null. Multiple matches always fail. Never replace a failed unique lookup with the first record of a page.
5. For join, supply columns, right_columns, paired left_keys/right_keys, join_kind=inner or left, cardinality=one_to_one or many_to_one, and null_keys=reject or never_match. Right keys must be unique. Keys use exact typed equality: numeric 1 and 1.0 match, text codes and booleans stay distinct. No fuzzy matching, many-to-many expansion or implicit duplicate aggregation is available.
6. For reconcile or variance, supply paired left_keys/right_keys, left_amount/right_amount and null_keys. Both sides must have unique keys. All records participate in a full outer comparison before pagination. Missing sides stay left_only/right_only, never zero. Duplicate keys need a business decision or a separately prepared source table; do not silently drop or aggregate them.
7. Financial amount operations require policies: numeric_text (reject/decimal), null_amounts (reject/exclude), unit, left_unit_column and right_unit_column. A unit column, when supplied, must match the declared unit on every record. A null unit-column setting is a caller declaration, not verified currency evidence. Use it only when the user or source convention establishes the unit. Invalid operands always reject; excluded blank amounts are counted and never treated as zero. Plain decimal text excludes grouping separators, currency symbols and formula expressions. If business policy is missing, ask before choosing it.
8. Reconcile requires a non-negative absolute tolerance. difference is left minus right; matched means its absolute value is at most tolerance. Report missing records, differing records and excluded amounts along with matched records. The tolerance does not round or alter the difference.
9. Variance requires direction=left_minus_right or right_minus_left, zero_baseline=reject or null, percentage_places (0 to 12), and rounding=ROUND_HALF_EVEN, ROUND_HALF_UP or ROUND_DOWN. The percentage is difference divided by the signed subtracted side, times 100. ROUND_DOWN rounds toward zero. Confirm this convention fits the business request, especially for negative baselines. Missing or excluded records have no percentage. Null zero-baseline percentages carry zero_baseline status.
10. Aging requires amount, due_date, as_of as YYYY-MM-DD, increasing positive bucket_days upper bounds, and policies with right_unit_column=null. Supply outstanding signed balances; the tool does not infer payments, open status or invoice totals. Dates are calendar dates, not timestamps. not_due and due_today are separate buckets; overdue upper bounds are inclusive, and a final bucket contains older records. Negative balances retain their signs. Excluded null amounts are counted. Empty buckets show zero with record_count=0.
11. Preserve labels, source table/column IDs, unit declarations, applied query and source revisions when explaining evidence. Never call a truncated record page a complete reconciliation. Arithmetic is exact within 64 significant digits and bounded decimal exponents; percentages round once under the explicit policy. A precision or output-budget error requires a narrower supported query or a business decision, not model arithmetic.
12. If ownership or revisions change, inspect again and refresh stale catalogue metadata through checked authoring when available. These tools read whole JSONB sheets before selecting registered ranges, with at most 10,000 source rows and a 65,536-byte response budget. They do not write cells, evaluate formulas, convert currencies or establish that the final prose has the correct metric labels.
